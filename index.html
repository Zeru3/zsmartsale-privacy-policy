# ZSmartSale Privacy Policy **Effective date:** November 19, 2026 ## 1. Introduction ZSmartSale is an offline business management application designed to help small and medium-sized businesses manage sales, inventory, expenses, customer credit transactions, and business records. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting information stored through the application. ## 2. Information Stored by the Application ZSmartSale stores business information locally on your Android device. This may include: - Business and company information - Product and inventory records - Sales and transaction records - Customer information - Expense records - Credit sales and outstanding balances - User accounts and application activity records ## 3. How Information Is Used The information stored in ZSmartSale is used to provide the application's business management functions, including sales recording, inventory management, expense tracking, reporting, and user management. ## 4. Data Collection and Sharing ZSmartSale operates offline and does not transmit business records to an online server for storage or license verification. We do not sell or share locally stored business records with third parties. ## 5. Data Storage and Security Business records are stored locally on the device. Users are responsible for protecting their devices and maintaining appropriate backups of important business information. Uninstalling the application or losing the device may result in the loss of locally stored information. ## 6. Data Retention and Deletion Users can manage or delete information through the application where those functions are available. Uninstalling the application may remove locally stored application data. ## 7. Children's Privacy ZSmartSale is a business management application and is not designed specifically for children. ## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy when application features or privacy practices change. Updated versions will be published on this webpage. ## 9. Contact Information For questions about this Privacy Policy or ZSmartSale, please contact the application developer through the contact information provided on the Google Play Store listing. **ZSmartSale – Business Management Made Simple**
